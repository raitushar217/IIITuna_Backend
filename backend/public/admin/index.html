IIIT Una Stream Elective Specialization Engine
Semesters 1 to 8 Dynamic Flow

In adherence with the IIIT Una academic regulations, students take unified institute foundational requirements in Semesters 1–4. At Semester 5, students select a specialization stream track. The elective choice in Semester 5 acts as an irreversible lock: subsequent electives in Semesters 6, 7, and 8 are constrained to that discipline.
